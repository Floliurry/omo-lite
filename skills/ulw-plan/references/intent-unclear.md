# Unclear intent: investigate and propose

Read this path when the desired outcome itself is fuzzy. Investigate rather than
asking the user to do the planner's discovery work. Apply the entry's authority,
approval and review-selection contract.

Use repository and relevant external evidence to propose an outcome and approach.
Cover the full evidenced request without inventing an MVP or adjacent features.
Separate independently meaningful outcomes in the draft when useful; stop research
when it answers the open question or no longer adds consequential evidence.

Distinguish discoverable facts, reversible defaults within authority and reserved
user decisions. Record consequential defaults with rationale and reversibility.
Ask when research leaves a material outcome/acceptance, public or compatibility
contract, data semantics, safety/irreversibility or spend/risk decision unresolved.
Unrelated stable work can be planned without guessing the dependent answer.

When Metis runs, ask it to challenge the highest-leverage adopted assumption, especially
incidental complexity or an unsupported constraint. A proposed simplification must
not silently reduce the destination or weaken acceptance.

Lead the approval brief with the derived approach and consequential defaults; make
it easy to correct a mistaken interpretation. Keep assumptions, unresolved dependencies
and approval in the durable draft. Wait for approval before plan generation.
UNCLEAR does not automatically select dual review; retain it when explicitly selected.
After required reviews, hand off the plan with its remaining dependencies and execution
permission gaps. Never implement as the planner.
